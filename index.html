About

More than a workout

Courtside Wellness was created around a simple idea: women deserve a place to move, get healthy, and be known. Our classes are private, women-only sessions held inside Padel Spot Minto, a co-ed facility, but our program is a space just for you. Come alone, and you won't stay a stranger for long.
