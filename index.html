About

Wellness at Courtside

Courtside Wellness is a women's fitness and wellness community, hosted inside Padel Spot Minto at 5 Saggart Field Road, Minto NSW 2566. Whatever brings you in — pilates, fitness, or just a reason to show up and move with people who know your name — classes run in small groups so there's room for real instruction, not just a room full of mats. Kids Fitness is open to all children.
