Our Program

A women's fitness and wellness community. Wear something you can move in and we'll take care of the rest — mats provided. Every class runs Monday to Friday.

Mat Pilates

10:00am · Mon–Fri · $20 · Women's

Small-group mat-based pilates for every body. Build strength, flexibility and control at your own pace.

Mums and Bubs

11:00am · Mon–Fri · $20 · Women's

Pilates designed for new mums, bub welcome in the room. A gentle way back to movement.

Kids Fitness

10:00am & 11:00am · Mon–Fri · $15 · Open to all kids

Movement and fitness fundamentals for kids, built to be active, fun, and easy to jump into.

Fitness Class

12:30pm · Mon–Fri · $20 · Women's

General fitness session for all levels, run alongside our pilates timetable.

Mat Pilates

  • Women's class — a space to build strength and confidence together.
  • 40-minute sessions — long enough to work, short enough to fit before work or school pickup.
  • Class size up to 30 — small enough that the instructor still knows your name.
  • Minimum 8 to run — if a class doesn't reach 8 people booked, we'll let everyone know and help you move to another session.
  • Pay as you go — no lock-in contract.
